\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Logo.} Wait for the DiHalt Studio fanfare to finish.
\item \textbf{Choose a language.} If prompted, plug an MSX mouse into port~1 and the languages will appear. Select \texttt{ESPA\~NOL} or \texttt{ENGLISH} with \key{$\leftarrow$}, \key{$\rightarrow$} or the mouse. Confirm with \key{SPACE}, \key{RETURN} or a click. At either prompt, \key{ESC} returns to MSX-DOS.
\item \textbf{Introduction.} After loading, the Lords of Waterdeep give you your mission. Press any key or click to skip the introduction.
\item \textbf{Title screen.} A menu with three choices:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{LOAD GAME IN PROGRESS}: goes on with the saved game (if there is none, play begins with a predefined party);
  \item \texttt{START A NEW PARTY}: a new game: first the party is made (section 6);
  \item \texttt{EXIT TO DOS}: back to MSX-DOS.
  \end{itemize}
  Choose with \key{$\uparrow$} \key{$\downarrow$} and confirm with \key{SPACE} or \key{RETURN}, or click the choice with the mouse.
\item \textbf{Character generation} (only for a new game). You make the four adventurers of the party; see section 6.
\item \textbf{The game.} The party starts at the entrance of the sewers, under the city.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{If the whole party falls}, the game loads your last save or returns to MSX-DOS if there is none. \textbf{If you defeat Xanathar}, you see the end of the story and return to MSX-DOS.

\textbf{To leave the adventure}, use \texttt{CAMP} $\to$ \texttt{Game Options} $\to$ \texttt{Quit Game} and choose \texttt{Yes}. Save first if you want to keep your progress.

The screen may go black for a few seconds while a new floor loads.
