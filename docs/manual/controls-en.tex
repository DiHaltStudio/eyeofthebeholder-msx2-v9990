The game is played as the original: \textbf{the mouse does everything}, and the keyboard is a shortcut for moving and attacking. With the mouse, the \textbf{left button takes and puts} items and presses buttons; the \textbf{right button uses} what a character holds in a hand.

Take or put the item of a hand & --- & left click on the hand\\
Pick up an item from the floor & --- & left click on it, in the lower half of the view\\

A character's inventory & --- & left click on the portrait\\

\textbf{Notes.} Holding a movement key repeats the step. An item you take with the mouse becomes the pointer: carry it to wherever you want to put it (an inventory box, the floor, another character). On the title screen, \key{$\uparrow$} \key{$\downarrow$} choose and \key{SPACE} or \key{RETURN} confirm.
