\sect{16. Bestiary}
These entries cover the creatures you can encounter across the twelve floors. \textbf{HP} gives the range of starting hit points and \textbf{AC} is armor class. Lower values make a creature harder to hit.

\textbf{Damage} gives the roll for each successful melee hit. Magic evasion is the approximate chance of avoiding a spell that checks that defense. Saving throws and the target's creature type also matter. Undead creatures resist Stinking Cloud, and clerics can attempt to turn them.
